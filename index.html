Quantum OPS Business Tracker Engine

The Problem

Small-to-medium business owners struggle with fragmented records—using paper logs, WhatsApp messages, and detached spreadsheets leading to inventory shrinkage and unknown net profit margins.

The Solution

A zero-latency web dashboard connecting front-end POS sales directly to instant inventory recalculations, instant profit margin calculation, and low-stock alerts.

The Tech Stack

Vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JS for maximum speed and longevity, paired with Supabase for real-time relational table listeners and Row Level Security (RLS).
